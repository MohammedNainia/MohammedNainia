WOULD YOU LIVE FOREVER?: PaperLit · Questions in Paper #7
Setting: a gothic castle balcony under a full moon. An ageless count holds out a glowing cup (hook-first format)
Voice: Samara X (Eleven v3, British). "Charmed" was removed from Artlist; Samara is the closest match (pitch 160 vs 165 Hz). One generation per line.

Line 1: [low, intrigued] Drink this, and you will never die.
Line 2: [thoughtful] He said yes. [pause] Five hundred years ago.
Line 3: [warmly] He has read every book. Seen every sunrise. Learned every language.
Line 4: [softly] But everyone he loved... grew old. [pause] One by one, their lights went out.
Line 5: [thoughtful] Maybe an ending is what makes each day count.
Line 6: [warmly] Or maybe forever is just more time to love again.
Line 7: [whispers] So... would you drink it?

Question left open: if you could never die, but everyone you love still would, would you choose forever?
