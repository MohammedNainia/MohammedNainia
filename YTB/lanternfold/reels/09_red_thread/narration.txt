WOULD YOU FOLLOW THE RED THREAD?: PaperLit · Questions in Paper #9
Setting: a firefly meadow at night. A red thread tied to your finger runs across the fields to the one you're meant to meet.
Based on the old East Asian legend of the red thread of fate.
Voice: Samara X (Eleven v3, British). One generation per line.

Line 1: [softly] Your soulmate is at the end of this thread.
Line 2: [intrigued] Follow it today... and you'll meet them tonight.
Line 3: [thoughtful] But you'd skip every wrong turn. Every almost. Every story on the way.
Line 4: [warmly] Maybe those detours are what make you ready.
Line 5: [softly] Or maybe waiting is just time you never get back.
Line 6: [whispers] So... would you follow it?

Question left open: if you could go straight to the person you're meant to be with, would you skip the journey?
