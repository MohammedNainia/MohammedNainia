WOULD YOU UNDO YOUR BIGGEST REGRET?: PaperLit · Questions in Paper #8
Setting: a stone ledge by the sea at dusk. Someone holds an old pocket watch that turns back time (hook-first format)
Voice: Samara X (Eleven v3, British). One generation per line.

Line 1: [softly] This old watch can turn back time.
Line 2: [thoughtful] One turn... and your biggest regret never happened.
Line 3: [warmly] The words you said. The chance you missed. Gone.
Line 4: [softly] But everything it taught you... would be gone too.
Line 5: [thoughtful] Maybe that pain is part of who you are now.
Line 6: [warmly] Or maybe you deserve a life without it.
Line 7: [whispers] So... would you turn it?

Question left open: if you could erase your biggest regret, but also lose everything it taught you, would you?
