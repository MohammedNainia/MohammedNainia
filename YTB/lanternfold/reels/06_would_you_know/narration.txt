WOULD YOU WANT TO KNOW THE DAY YOU DIE?: PaperLit · Questions in Paper #6
Setting: desert under a big moon (hook-first format)
Voice: Charmed (Eleven v3, British), stability 0.5. One generation per line.

Line 1: [softly] Imagine someone hands you an envelope.
Line 2: [thoughtful] Inside is one date. [pause] The day your life ends.
Line 3: [warmly] Maybe you'd stop waiting. [pause] Say the things. Live every day on purpose.
Line 4: [softly] Or maybe... not knowing is what lets you breathe.
Line 5: [calm] One life counts its days. [pause] The other never counts at all.
Line 6: [thoughtful] Neither is wrong. [pause] Both take courage.
Line 7: [whispers] So... would you open it?

Question left open: If you could know the exact day your life ends, would you want to?
