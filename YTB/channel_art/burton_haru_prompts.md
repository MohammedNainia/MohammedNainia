# Artlist prompts: Haru as a Tim Burton style stop-motion puppet

Use these in Artlist (Nano Banana or the best image model you have). For every prompt, **attach the current Haru
image as the character reference** (`YTB/channel_art/haru_ref.png`, or the blank-face Haru generation
`01a20472-a03b-705e-beec-2791c8c5c082`) so she stays recognisable: long straight black hair with a side fringe,
mustard hoodie, charcoal trousers, white sneakers.

Generate 4 or more of each, pick the best, then use the chosen turnaround as the reference for prompts 2 to 5 so
the whole set matches. Save the picks in `YTB/channel_art/` as `burton_turnaround.png`, `burton_expressions.png`,
`burton_poses.png`, `burton_materials.png`, `burton_mood.png`.

---

## 1. Turnaround sheet (the main one; I measure the puppet against it)

Aspect ratio 16:9.

> Character turnaround sheet of a stop-motion animation puppet in the style of Tim Burton's Corpse Bride and The
> Nightmare Before Christmas. The character is Haru, a pale young girl about 10 years old. Burton proportions: a
> large slightly oval head on a thin long neck, a slender body, long thin arms and legs, spindly fingers, small
> feet. Big round dark eyes with soft grey-purple shadows under them, thin dark eyebrows, a tiny nose, a small
> simple mouth, no blush. Long straight jet-black hair past the shoulders with a side-swept fringe, sculpted with
> fine strand grooves. She wears an oversized mustard-ochre hoodie made of real knitted fabric with a kangaroo
> pocket, two drawstrings and soft folds, charcoal grey slim trousers of real woven fabric, and small white canvas
> sneakers with thin grey rubber soles. Real puppet materials: smooth matte silicone skin with slight translucency,
> real fabric clothes with visible weave and fuzz, handmade miniature quality. Five full-body views in one row,
> same scale, feet on one line: front, three-quarter front, side, three-quarter back, back. Neutral standing pose,
> arms relaxed at her sides. Plain light grey seamless studio background, soft even studio lighting, no text, no
> labels.

Tips
- If her face drifts away from Haru between generations, re-attach the chosen turnaround and add "same character as
  the reference image" at the start of the prompt.
- If the model makes her look like a real child or a CG cartoon, add "photograph of a handmade stop-motion puppet,
  not CGI, not a real person".
- If the background isn't plain, add "isolated on a plain light grey seamless background" at the end.
