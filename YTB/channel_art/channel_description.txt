PaperLit channel description (Haru / dark clay stop-motion concept)

Every story has a rule. Haru always breaks it.

Haru is a little girl made of clay, and nothing in her world is safe: the tooth fairy, the magic lamp, the book in the attic, the family at the dinner table. Each short is one complete horror story in dark stop-motion clay, told in her own words, with a rule, a mistake, and an ending that doesn't let go.

New scary story every week. Watch to the end. Then watch it again; the first second always means something different the second time.

Turn the light off. Haru's waiting.
