[whispering] The last keeper left me three rules. [nervous] I broke one on the very first night.

[calm] The boat dropped me on the island at six. It wouldn't come back for a week. Just me... the sea... and the lighthouse.

[thoughtful] Pinned next to the lamp, there was a note. Rule one. Keep the light turning, all night. Rule two. Never count the people on the rocks. Rule three. If someone knocks... don't answer. [whispering] They don't knock.

[laughs nervously] I laughed. There are no people on the rocks. It's an island. There's nobody here but me.

[calm] The first hours were quiet. Just the waves... and the light, going round... and round... and round.

[nervous] Then, on one sweep... I saw someone. Standing on the rocks. Facing the tower. [whispering] Not moving.

The light came round again. Two of them. [breathing heavily] I didn't mean to count. It just... happens. Three. Four.

[scared] Every time the beam came back, there was one more. And they were closer. Five. Six. [panicked] Seven. Eight.

[shaky voice] The ninth one was standing at the bottom of my stairs.

[whispering] Rule two. Never count the people on the rocks. So I shut my eyes. I pressed my hands over them. And I stopped counting.

[breathing heavily] I don't know how long I sat there. Listening to the light... going round.

[whispering] When I opened my eyes... they were gone. All of them. [relieved sigh] The rocks were empty.

[calm] I sat down next to the lamp. I told myself it was over.

[whispering] Then someone knocked on the door.

[shaky voice] Rule three. If someone knocks, don't answer. They don't knock. [panicked] So who was knocking?

[whispering] Then a voice, right behind the door... said my name.

[gasps] And the light... stopped turning.
