THANKSGIVING v2, scarier (9:16, target 90-100 s, dark clay, one line per voice generation)
Hook text at frame 0: MY FAMILY FATTENED ME / FOR THE THING / UNDER THE TABLE.
Rule: never open the cellar on Thanksgiving. Never look under the tablecloth.
Haru's choice: she hides food in her napkin, and she looks under the tablecloth.
Twist: it was under the table the whole dinner, touching her ankles. The family never ate; they watched her eat.
Ending/loop: next Thanksgiving, Haru's chair is empty; Grandma fills little cousin Mei's plate.

h01  HARU     My family fattened me for the thing under the table.
              [frame 0: Haru at the table, plate piled high; the tablecloth hangs to the floor; something shifts under it.]
h02  HARU     All Thanksgiving, they watched me eat. Nobody else touched their food.
              [the family around the table, hands folded, plates clean, smiling at her.]
g01  GRANDMA  Eat, sweetheart. You're too thin.
h03  HARU     Something brushed my ankle under the table. I thought it was the dog.
h04  HARU     We don't have a dog.
              [close: under the tablecloth, long grey fingers slide over her sock.]
p01  GRANDPA  We thank you for the harvest. She's almost ripe.
              [grace: the family bows, but they're bowing to the floor.]
h05  HARU     My cousin Aki used to sit across from me. Two years ago, they said he moved away.
              [an empty chair across the table, a plate set anyway. Teeth marks on the chair leg.]
h06  HARU     I hid my food in my napkin.
g02  GRANDMA  You can't hide it from him, sweetheart. He can smell how much is left.
              [Grandma takes the napkin, opens it, scrapes the food back onto Haru's plate.]
h07  HARU     After the pie, I ran for the front door. It was locked.
h08  HARU     Nobody chased me. They just sat back down.
              [the family at the table, calm, in the warm light; Haru at the dark door behind them.]
h09  HARU     The tablecloth started to rise.
              [the cloth lifts off the floor like a tent. Plates slide. Candles tip.]
t01  IT       Is she ready?
g03  GRANDMA  She's ready.
              [Haru's chair scrapes backward on its own, toward the table. Her nails drag on the floor. She goes under the cloth.
               Black. Under it all: a muffled scream, cutlery.]
h10  HARU     And then, for the first time all day, my family picked up their forks.
              [the family, smiling, lifting knives and forks. Cut to black.]
              [next Thanksgiving: a new car outside, the table richer. Haru's chair empty, a plate set anyway.
               Grandma spoons potatoes onto little cousin Mei's plate.]
g04  GRANDMA  Eat, sweetheart. You're too thin.
              [the tablecloth twitches. Cut to frame 0 (loop).]
