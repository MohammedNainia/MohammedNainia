THANKSGIVING (9:16, target 85-95 s, dark clay, one line per voice generation)
Hook text at frame 0: MY FAMILY KEPT / FEEDING ME. / NOW I KNOW WHY.
Rule: never open the cellar door on Thanksgiving.
Haru's choice: the knocking under the floor; she opens the cellar while everyone sleeps.
Twist: every Thanksgiving the family feeds one child for the thing under the house. Cousin Aki "moved away" two years ago.
Ending/loop: next Thanksgiving, Haru's chair is empty; Grandma fills little cousin Mei's plate: "Eat, sweetheart. You're too thin."
Unspoken question: Mei is next. Who was circled before Aki?

h01  HARU     My family kept feeding me all Thanksgiving. Now I know why.
              [frame 0: Haru at the table, plate piled high, Grandma's hand spooning more on.]
h02  HARU     Every plate I emptied, Grandma filled again.
g01  GRANDMA  Eat, sweetheart. You're too thin.
h03  HARU     Under the floor, something knocked. Three times.
              [the table goes quiet. Glasses tremble. Grandpa taps his foot twice.]
p01  GRANDPA  Not yet.
h04  HARU     There was one rule in that house. Never open the cellar on Thanksgiving.
h05  HARU     My cousin Aki used to sit across from me. Two years ago, they said he moved away.
              [an empty chair across the table, a plate set in front of it anyway.]
h06  HARU     After the pie, Grandma squeezed my arm, the way she checks fruit.
g02  GRANDMA  Almost.
h07  HARU     When everyone fell asleep on the couch, I went to the cellar door. It was warm, like breath.
h08  HARU     I opened it.
              [the cellar walls: Thanksgiving photos, one per year, one child circled in red in each. Aki, last year.]
h09  HARU     A child was circled in every photo. Aki was last year.
              [a new photo, still wet: this year, Haru circled.]
              [from the dark, a mouth unfolds, wide as the cellar, a napkin tucked under its chin.]
t01  IT       Is she ready?
h10  HARU     Behind me, my whole family stood on the stairs.
g03  GRANDMA  She's ready.
              [black. A dinner bell. Knives on plates.]
              [next Thanksgiving: the family smiling, a new car outside. Haru's chair empty, a plate set anyway.
               Grandma spoons potatoes onto little cousin Mei's plate.]
g04  GRANDMA  Eat, sweetheart. You're too thin.
              [cut to frame 0 (loop).]
