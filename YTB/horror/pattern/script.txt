THE PATTERN (draft 1, approved)  9:16, voices 1.5x in post (pitch kept), target ~75 s
Hook text at frame 0: I CAME HOME FROM EGYPT. / EVERYTHING LOOKED THE SAME. / I NEVER LEFT THE PYRAMID.
Rule: walk the guide's pattern inside the pyramid: five slow steps, two fast, never break it (never explained).  Flaw: Haru's curiosity; she breaks it on purpose.
Twist: home was only what the room showed her to keep her still; she never left, and she has taken the goat's place in the circle.
Ending: a hood slides back: Mum, smiling too wide. "Welcome home, Haru." Black. Loop to the hook.
Voices: Haru = Samara X (stability .5); the guide = Shadow (calm, low); Mum = Assistance. The gasp is Haru's voice.
