THE VACANCY (draft 1, approved)  9:16, voices 1.5x in post (pitch kept), target ~75 s
Hook text at frame 0: THREE OF US CHECKED IN. / THREE OF US LEFT. / I WASN'T ONE OF THEM.
Rule: in the dark they are wrong; in the lamplight they are people.  Flaw: Haru's curiosity (she goes down into the dark to see).
Twist: the thing that took her place is already standing there, wearing her.  Ending: black, the sign clicks to NO VACANCY. Loop to the hook.
Voices: Haru = Samara X (stability .5); the other Haru = the same voice, slowed and doubled in post.

h01  HARU   Three of us checked into that motel. Three of us left. I wasn't one of them.
h02  HARU   We'd been driving for hours when Dad saw the sign. Vacancy. Nothing else for fifty miles.
h03  HARU   Nobody was at the desk. Our key was already on the counter, with our name on the tag.
h04  HARU   The bed in our room was still warm. Mom said it was the heater. There was no heater.
h05  HARU   I couldn't breathe in there, so I went out on the balcony.
h06  HARU   Something came out of the trees on all fours. Its legs bent in too many places.
h07  HARU   It walked into the lamplight, and it was a man. A tired man with a suitcase.
h08  HARU   Then a woman, her head hanging sideways off her neck, until the light set it straight.
h09  HARU   Then a little boy. He looked up at me and waved.
h10  HARU   They all had keys. There were no other cars.
h11  HARU   I gripped the railing until the paint flaked off on my palms. I should have gone inside.
h12  HARU   I went down into the dark instead. I had to see what they really looked like.
h13  HARU   Behind the motel, the ground was open. Something was climbing out, still choosing a face.
h14  HARU   I turned to run back to Mom and Dad.
h15  HARU   I was already there.
d01  DOUBLE Don't worry. I'll tell them.
