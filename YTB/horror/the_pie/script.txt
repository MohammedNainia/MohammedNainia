THE PIE  (about 80 to 90 s, 9:16, English, visual-first)
Rule: it only moves when nobody is looking at it. Sound motif: the smell is shown as steam; slow heavy footsteps; an old woman's breathing.
Haru = yellow hoodie, long black hair. Sana = short black bob with a fringe, red cardigan, ribbon clip (never mistaken for Haru).
The grandmother is never seen. Pink italic = anyone who isn't Haru.

HOOK  (frame 0: Sana's house at night, one warm kitchen window, steam curling from it)
HARU (whisper, and on screen): I should've never gone to her house.

1. ARRIVAL (evening, warm kitchen light)
Haru sniffs the air.  HARU: Something smells amazing.
SANA: Grandma's making her apple and cinnamon pie. It's for later.
Close-up: the oven glowing orange. Nobody standing at it. A wooden spoon still turning in a bowl, slowing to a stop.

2. THE FOOTSTEPS (living room; the dark hallway behind them)
Slow, heavy footsteps cross the hallway. Creak. Creak.
HARU: Is that your grandma?
SANA (eating, not looking up, not looking at the hallway): I didn't hear anything.

3. THE SELFIE (setup: the phone sees what Haru can't)
Haru takes a selfie of them both. On the phone screen, something dark crosses fast behind them in the hallway.
Haru spins round: empty. She checks the photo: just the two of them, and the hallway door, now open.

4. NIGHT (blue light, Sana asleep)
HARU (whisper): Sana... what about the pie?   Sana doesn't answer. The clock: 2:47.
HARU (narration): The pie never came. But the smell never left.

5. THE HALLWAY (Haru's choice)
Haru gets up for the toilet. The toilet door is right there, on the left.
At the far end of the hallway, under a closed door: warm light, and the smell, and steam.
She walks past the toilet, toward it.  (her choice: curiosity)
Footsteps behind her. She turns: nothing.
She raises the phone in selfie mode to watch behind her while she walks (payoff of the selfie):
 - on the screen, far back in the dark: a shape that wasn't there.
 - she takes three steps: on the screen, it is halfway down the hallway.
 - one more step: on the screen, it fills the frame behind her shoulder. The phone shows only darkness and something breathing.
Behind her ear: an old woman's slow breath.

6. CAUGHT
Haru lowers the phone and turns, with her own eyes.
Close-up on her face: eyes wide. We never see what she sees.
The camera RUSHES at her face from its point of view, impossibly fast, a roar of air. Cut to black. Silence.

7. THE NEXT MORNING (bright, birds)
Sana walks to school alone, schoolbag on, the bob and the red cardigan clear. She does not look back.
The camera stays on the house. On the kitchen windowsill an apple pie is cooling. One slice is missing.
(Loop: the window dissolves to night, to the hook shot.)
