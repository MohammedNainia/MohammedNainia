THE TOOTH FAIRY (9:16, target 85-95 s, one line per voice generation)
Hook text at frame 0: THE TOOTH FAIRY / TOOK ALL / MY TEETH.
Rule: tooth under the pillow = a coin. Eyes shut till morning. If you open them, get to the light.
Haru's choice: she wants the pretty fairy from her books, pulls the next tooth herself, and opens her eyes.
Twist: the light holds until every light in the house goes out. It pulls her teeth one by one and pays for each. Mum's teeth are in a glass: it happened to her too.
Unspoken question: who turned the lights off, and why does Mum already have a glass ready?

h01  HARU  The tooth fairy took all my teeth.
           [frame 0: close on Haru, lips pressed shut. On "teeth" she opens them a little: empty gums. Hook text.]
h02  HARU  My tooth came out at dinner. Mum didn't smile. She never smiles with her mouth open.
           [a small bloody tooth on a plate. Mum, lips pressed shut.]
m01  MUM   Under your pillow. Eyes shut till morning. And if you ever open them, get to the light.
h03  HARU  In the morning there was a coin. It was cold, and something had bitten it.
           [close: an old coin with tiny bite marks.]
h04  HARU  I wanted to see her. The fairy from my books, with the glitter wings.
h05  HARU  So I wiggled the next tooth out myself.
           [Haru in the mirror, pulling. Wince. Tooth in her palm, smiling.]
h06  HARU  That night something came in. It clicked, like teeth shaken in a jar.
           [dark bedroom, eyes shut. Door creaks. Shadow with torn paper wings.]
h07  HARU  Its fingers slid under my pillow, and kept sliding.
           [long grey fingers, too many joints, under the pillow beside her cheek.]
h08  HARU  I opened my eyes.
           [her POV: a face inches above, a huge grin full of mismatched children's teeth. It says nothing. It smiles wider.]
h09  HARU  I ran for the hall light. It stopped at the edge, like the light was deep water.
           [Haru in the lit hallway. Fingers reach in from the dark, sizzle, pull back.]
h10  HARU  I locked myself in the bathroom, under the brightest bulb in the house.
h11  HARU  It tapped on the door all night. Then every light in the house went out.
           [the bulb buzzes and dies. Black. The lock turns by itself. The door creaks open.]
c01  IT    Open wide.
           [the bulb flickers back for one last second: the creature is crouched on top of Haru on the bathroom floor, wings spread, its long fingers on her chin, its grin right over her face. Haru's eyes wide. The bulb dies.]
           [black screen. A wet pull, a coin drops on tile. Again. Again. Faster, one by one. Then silence.]
h12  HARU  It paid for every single one.
           [morning: the bathroom floor covered in bitten coins. Haru at the mirror, lips shut. She opens them: no teeth.]
           [last shot, no line: Mum in the doorway sets a glass of water by Haru's bed. Cut to frame 0 (loop).]
