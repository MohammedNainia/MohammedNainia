THE MAGIC LAMP v2 (9:16, target 85-95 s, dark clay, one line per voice generation)
Hook text at frame 0: I RUBBED A MAGIC LAMP. / SOMETHING CAME OUT. / I WENT IN.
Rule: the old man at the market: look at it all you like, never rub it.
Haru's flaw/choice: she can't leave it alone. Three nights, then she rubs it for "just one wish".
Twist: there is no genie. The lamp is full of everyone who ever made a wish, and when you rub it, the one who has been
       inside longest climbs out wearing your face. The old man who sold it was one of them.
Ending/loop: the thing wearing Haru sells the lamp to the next child, with the same words. Cut to frame 0.
Unspoken question: who is the next child, and was anyone at that market ever who they look like?

h01  HARU     I rubbed a magic lamp. Something came out. I went in.
              [frame 0: inside the lamp. Haru, small, at the bottom of a brass throat, looking up. Far above, a round hole of
               warm light: her bedroom ceiling. A face leans over the hole and looks down at her. It is her own face.]
h02  HARU     The old man at the market sold it to me for one coin.
              [daytime market, clay stalls. A tiny old man in a green coat, his skin faintly metallic at the fingertips.]
s01  SELLER   Look at it all you like. Just never rub it.
              [he pushes the lamp across the cloth with one finger, as if it were hot.]
h03  HARU     I lasted three nights.
              [Haru's bedroom, night one: the lamp on the shelf. Night two: on her pillow. Night three: in her lap, her thumb
               hovering over the brass.]
h04  HARU     I only wanted one wish. Just one.
              [she rubs. The spout breathes in. The room tilts toward the lamp like water toward a drain; her hair, the
               curtains, her blanket all stream into the spout, then her.]
h05  HARU     It was warm in there. Like the inside of a mouth.
              [the interior: curved brass walls with ridges like a throat, sweating drops of oil. The floor gives under her
               feet. Somewhere, slow breathing that is not hers.]
h06  HARU     There was no genie. There were people.
              [her eyes adjust: the walls are covered in brass figures, fused into the metal at the back, green with age.
               Every one of them turns its eyes to her.]
h07  HARU     A man had wished to be rich. He was still coughing up coins.
              [a brass man on his knees, a heap of coins around him to the waist; he heaves, and another coin falls out.]
h08  HARU     A mother had wished her baby would never grow up. It never did.
              [a brass mother rocking a newborn-sized baby, as she has for three hundred years. Close on the baby: its eyes are
               old and tired, sunk in wrinkles. It looks at Haru, and its tiny mouth starts the whisper: make a wish.]
h09  HARU     A boy had wished never to be alone.
              [a boy in the middle of a ring of arms. Each arm grows out of the wall and ends in a hand holding him: his
               wrists, his hair, his mouth. Dozens.]
x01  WALLS    (whispers, many voices) Make a wish. Make a wish. Make a wish.
              [the figures' lips move together. Haru backs away and steps on something soft: a hand in the floor.]
h10  HARU     Then the oldest one stood up.
              [a girl in a dress three hundred years old peels herself out of the wall, flakes of brass falling off her. She
               walks past Haru without hurry, up the curve of the throat toward the light.]
h11  HARU     She had my face.
              [close: as she climbs into the light, the brass on her skin warms into clay pink. Her hair darkens and grows
               long. She turns once and smiles down. It is Haru's smile, almost.]
g01  GIRL     (Haru's own voice, slower) Thank you for the wish.
              [she climbs out of the spout. The hole of light shrinks to a coin.]
h12  HARU     She sleeps in my bed now. Mum hasn't noticed.
              [through the spout from below: the bedroom. Mum tucks the girl in and kisses her forehead. The girl looks past
               Mum, straight down into the lamp.]
m01  MUM      Night, Haru.
h13  HARU     Every Saturday she takes the lamp to the market.
              [the market again. Behind the old man's stall, a new seller: the girl in Haru's hoodie. A small boy in a
               striped shirt stops and stares at the brass.]
g02  GIRL     Look at it all you like. Just never rub it.
              [she pushes the lamp across the cloth with one finger. The boy's hand reaches for it. Cut to frame 0 (loop).]

VOICES: Haru = Samara X. Seller = an old man (new Artlist voice, not Grandpa). Girl = Samara X, same voice, slower and
flatter, a touch of reverb. Walls = "Hushed", layered several times. Mum = the tooth fairy mum voice.

WHAT WE BUILD FOR THE INSIDE OF THE LAMP (clay, dark, lit only from the hole above and the brass's own dull shine):
  - the throat: a tall curved brass chamber with ridges, oil sweat, a soft floor that dents under feet
  - the hole of light at the top (Haru's bedroom ceiling seen through the spout)
  - the coin man (kneeling, coin heap, coins dropping from his mouth on a loop)
  - the rocking mother and the baby that never grew up (old eyes, whispering mouth)
  - the held boy (a ring of wall arms holding him)
  - wall figures whose eyes follow Haru and whose lips move on the whispers
  - hands in the floor
  - the girl who climbs out: brass to clay, Haru's face
  - the market stall (day) and Haru's bedroom (night) from the existing set kit
