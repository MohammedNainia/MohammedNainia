Haru #6 "The Sunflower in Our Garden" (wordless, about 13.5 s, 9:16, Blender clay)
Follows "The Gathering". Set: Haru's bedroom upstairs (library/sets/home/upstairs.py, her bed under the window),
plus the back garden outside the window. New opening rules: bright frame 0, white hook line from frame 0,
a clear sound inside 0.5 s, loop back to frame 0.

HOOK LINE (white, frame 0 to 2.2 s):
  "The sunflower in our garden was facing my window."

0.0  FRAME 0. Inside the bedroom, warm bedside lamp on. Haru sits up in bed, back to the camera, left of frame.
     The window fills the right half: the sunflower's head is pressed close outside the glass, lit warm by her
     lamp, slanted void eyes, thin mouth. Bright and readable, the face big in frame.
0.2  SOUND: a soft "tok". The head taps the glass once. Crickets under everything.
1.0  Haru climbs out of bed, never taking her eyes off it. (Medium, from the bed's foot, 28 mm.)
3.0  Close-up of her hands on the curtains. A short breath.
4.0  She yanks the curtains shut. SOUND: curtain swish, then quiet.
5.0  WIDE from behind Haru: closed curtains lit from behind by the garden streetlamp. One big round shadow
     with petals sits on the curtain. Hold.
6.5  The shadow head tilts slowly sideways, like a dog listening. SOUND: a dry creak of the stem.
8.5  Haru takes one step back.
9.5  Two more shadows rise slowly beside it from below the sill: people, head and shoulders, each with a small
     candle glow. The villagers from The Gathering. SOUND: the far hummed chant comes back, very faint.
11.5 All three shadows tilt their heads at the same moment. The hum stops dead.
12.3 Close-up: Haru turns toward the camera, terrified (wide eyes).
13.5 HARD CUT to frame 0: the curtains are open again and the flower is at the glass. The loop.

NOTES
- No narration and no captions beyond the hook line.
- Sound: quiet and natural, about -18 LUFS. A tap, crickets, a swish, a creak, a breath, a faint hum.
- New set work: a back garden under the bedroom window with a giant sunflower (re-using the Gathering flower
  head) tall enough to reach the window, a streetlamp behind it for the shadows, and working curtains.
- Avoids "it gets closer every time": the flower never moves closer; the scare is the others joining it.
