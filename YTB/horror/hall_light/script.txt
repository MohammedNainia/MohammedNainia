Haru #8 "The Hall Light" (NARRATED, about 40 s, 9:16, Blender clay)

Set: Haru's bedroom at night (the same room as Lights Off and Don't Open). Her bedside lamp, her bed facing her bedroom
door, the upstairs hallway behind it with a ceiling bulb on a wall switch right outside her door. Built with the kits:
the door with the hallway lit behind it (openings.py), and the lamp, bulb and switch (practicals.py). The clay key on
every close-up and medium shot.
Opening rules: bright frame 0, white hook line from frame 0, a sound and her voice inside 0.5 s, loop back to frame 0.

  "If the hall light is on after midnight,"
