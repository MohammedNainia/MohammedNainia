h05  HARU   I turned around. Nothing. In the glass, the extra one was standing closer.
h06  HARU   It had no face. Just the back of a head.
h07  HARU   The next morning, seven more were facing the walls. By lunch, fourteen. All facing the same way.
h08  HARU   Toward the empty classroom at the end of the third floor.
h09  HARU   I always have to know. So that evening, I went in.
h10  HARU   One wall was covered in scratches. Names. Dates. And one sentence, over and over.
h11  HARU   Don't let it turn around.
h12  HARU   Something was breathing behind the plaster. I pressed my ear to it. Something pressed back.
x01  WALL   Thank you for looking.

h14  HARU   Its head began to turn. I ran.

h17  HARU   It can only see through the eyes that look at it. They weren't hiding from it. They were keeping it blind.
