WOULD YOU LISTEN?: Questions in Paper #10
Setting: an autumn forest at golden hour. Two people on a bench. Above the other person's head, a sealed paper thought-bubble that you could open.
Voice: Samara X (Eleven v3, British). One generation per line.

Line 1: [softly] You can hear exactly what they think of you.
Line 2: [intrigued] Every thought. No filter. Just open it.
Line 3: [thoughtful] But what if it's not what you hoped?
Line 4: [warmly] Knowing could make you closer than ever.
Line 5: [softly] Or break something you can never unhear.
Line 6: [thoughtful] Some would need to know. Some never could. [pause] Which one are you?

Question left open: would you want to hear exactly what the person you love thinks of you?
