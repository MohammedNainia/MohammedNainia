# ALLO FLOUSSI — "The Moments That Mustn't Stop"
### 60-second cinematic film · Screenplay, shot list, cast, palette, music · v1 (for approval)

**Format:** 60s · 16:9 master (9:16 safe-framed) · 24 fps · 1080p
**Cities:** Casablanca (mother + couple) and Rabat (friends)
**Tagline:** ما تخليش الصرف يوقف لحظتك. — *Small change. Big moments.*
**Subtitles:** French + English (burned-in versions of each)

---

## 1. The idea in one line

Three people in two cities, three small moments, and one familiar sentence stops all of them: **"ما عنديش الصرف."**
Then all three say **"ألو؟"**, and one rider in green and blue gets all three moments going again.

**Visual motif:** the logo's two interlocking rings (green + blue, with a smile). The motif shows up in the film three ways:
- as circles: a coin, a café saucer, the rider's wheel
- in the final transition, where the rings interlock
- in the end-card animation

## 7. How each element will be made

| Element | Method |
|---|---|
| Characters and locations | AI reference sheets first. Every video shot is then generated *from* those references, which keeps faces, wardrobe and places the same across shots. |
| Video shots (19) | Image-to-video from approved storyboard stills. Chosen per shot from the cheapest models that give good 1080p results (Veo 3.1 Lite / Kling v3 Pro). Phase 2, after your credits renew on **Nov 28**. |
| App UI (shot 11) and logo animation (shot 19) | **Built as real motion graphics** (vector + code-rendered), not AI. Arabic text, prices and your logo come out accurate. |
| Banknotes | Framed partly and in shallow focus. AI can't render real dirham notes exactly, so no close-up shows a fake note. |
| Darija dialogue | Recorded as an AI voice performance per character and synced in the edit. Lines are short and mostly off-camera or in profile, so imperfect lip-sync won't show. |
| Music | Original score generated to the cue sheet above, then edited to picture, including the silences |
| SFX | Real recordings from Freesound plus Artlist's catalog |
| Edit, grade, subtitles, export | Assembled and graded here, with FR and EN subtitle versions and a 9:16 reframe |
